01 Interview We start in your kitchen — how you cook, store, gather. That conversation sets the brief.
02 Design Layout, materials and finishes are drawn up on the METOD base system and reviewed with you.
03 Fabrication Fronts, edges and countertops are built by hand in our Barcelona workshop.
04 Delivery Installed and finished on site, ready to use.
